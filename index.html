Signature features

What sets Chatter apart

Four features you will not find in every messaging app, shown with real screens from Chatter.

01 · Customize

Design the look of your chats

The Customization Studio lets you shape how Chatter looks, with a live chat preview so you see every change before you save.

  • Save your own themes and switch between them
  • Choose a font size: Small, Default, Large or Huge
  • Pick from five font styles, including Bricolage, Nunito and Lora
  • Set your tab colour, then undo or redo any change
02 · Message tags

Keep track of what matters in every chat

Message tags are labels for the messages in your direct chats. Create a tag, attach it to the messages you need, and come back to them in one place.

  • Create tags with your own name and one of eight colours
  • Put one tag on up to five messages
  • Search your tags and tap View to see what is inside
  • Hold a tag to edit it
03 · Confidential

Keep private chats behind their own lock

Confidential Chats has its own separate lock, so sensitive conversations stay away from your everyday inbox.

  • Open it with a Confidential PIN
  • Prefer a password? Switch to your Confidential password instead
  • Locked out? A "Forgot both?" option is one tap away
  • Inside, you get your own Chats, Notes, Search and Settings
04 · Ghost Canvas

Draw together on a Ghost Canvas

Sketch with someone in real time. Nothing opens until the other person says yes.

  • Pick who you want to draw with
  • They get an invitation, and the canvas opens only if they accept
  • Choose how to let them know: a quiet banner or a call-style ring

The essentials

Core features

Everything you need for everyday messaging, in one app. Chatter runs on Android, desktop and the web.

Messaging

Fast, reliable chats.

  • Direct and group chats
  • Edit or delete a message for everyone within 10 seconds
  • Delete a message for yourself only
  • Reactions, replies, @mentions and an emoji picker
  • Messages wait and send in order when you are back online (direct chats)
  • Chats are saved on your device, so they open fast

Calls and social

More ways than text.

  • Voice calls and group calls
  • Stories, polls and a built-in calendar
  • Ghost Canvas, a shared drawing space that opens only when the other person agrees
  • Contact requests, contacts and a blocked-contacts screen

Privacy and control

You choose who reaches you.

  • PIN lock with biometric unlock
  • Choose who can add, message or call you, and who sees your details
  • Private profile details are protected on the server
  • Delete chat history for yourself only

Inbox and personalisation

Make the app work the way you do.

  • Swipe a chat to reveal quick buttons: Pin and Archive on the left, Message and Call on the right
  • Change or turn off the swipe buttons in Settings
  • Pin, archive, mute and favourite chats
  • Appearance, chat style and notification preferences
